Camera Setup

I just use the Nikon Coolpix p1100 which has great zoom but no customizability and a small sensor so its slow and not great for low light,
it also has no weather sealing so I cant take it anywhere with a harsh climate. In the future I hope to upgrade to a mirrorless setup with a nice lens.
