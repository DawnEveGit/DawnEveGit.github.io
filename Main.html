Overview & Diversity

North America is home to roughly 1100 documented bird species. Whether you are watching backyards or national parks, birding is one of the fastest growing outdoor hobbies across the continent.

“In order to see birds it is necessary to become a part of the silence.” — Robert Lynd

Fun Facts

Here are a few cool facts about North American species:
Take note of migration ranges and speed records.

  • The Peregrine Falcon can dive at speeds over 200 mph (roughly 2.0 × 102 mph).
  • The Ruby-throated Hummingbird weighs less than a nickel (about mavg = 3.2 grams).
  • Warblers migrate thousands of miles each spring across the Gulf of Mexico.
